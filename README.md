# Audit Fee & AI Dividend Calculator

A single-page app in one file (`index.html`) with no dependencies. It's the companion tool for an edition of the LinkedIn newsletter **AI in Finance, Done Right**: **"KPMG Built an Army of Audit AI Agents—And Accidentally Fired the Opening Shot Against the Billable Hour"**.

## Features
- **Tab 1, Fee model:** Enter your audit fee, the share that is execution work, the AI reduction of that work, the added senior review, and the tech cost recovery. See next year's hours-based fee, the discount ceiling, the net AI saving and how much of it reaches the client. The KPMG/Grant Thornton -14% is shown as a reference, not a benchmark.
- **Tab 2, Client readiness:** Six gates covering clean ERP exports, standardized subledgers, full-population testing, earlier risk surfacing, governed AI use, and evidence of human skepticism. You get a negotiating-position dial, the biggest gaps, and a fee negotiation brief you can copy.

The demo figures are made up. Everything runs in the browser and nothing is uploaded.
